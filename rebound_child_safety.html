Child Safety Policy

Effective Date: October 30, 2026

Our Commitment

Rebound is a job search and career assistance application designed for adult job seekers. The safety and protection of children is of utmost importance to us. Rebound has a strict zero-tolerance policy against any content, activity, or behavior that sexually exploits or abuses minors (CSAE).

Prohibited Content and Activities

The following are strictly prohibited on the Rebound platform and in any content associated with the Rebound app:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) of any kind
  • Any content that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers minors
  • Grooming, solicitation, or predatory behavior targeting minors
  • Any attempt to communicate with minors for sexual purposes
  • Sharing, distributing, or linking to any material that exploits children

App Audience

Rebound is intended solely for users who are 18 years of age or older. The app provides job listings, resume tools, and interview preparation resources for adult professionals. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from or provide services to anyone under the age of 18.

Reporting

If you encounter any content on or related to the Rebound app that you believe involves the sexual exploitation or abuse of a minor, please report it immediately to:

  • Email: mpboopathi@gmail.com
  • National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC): CyberTipline

Enforcement

Any user found to be in violation of this policy will be immediately banned from the Rebound platform and reported to the appropriate law enforcement authorities. We cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations related to child safety.
