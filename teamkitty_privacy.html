TeamKitty Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

TeamKitty is a team-finances and scheduling app for small sports club teams. This page explains what information the app collects and how it's used, in plain language.

📋

What we collect

  • Account info: the email address and password you sign up with, and the display name you choose.
  • Team activity you enter: ledger entries (amounts, categories, notes, and optional receipt photos you upload), game and attendance records, and practice session details you or your teammates add.
  • Nothing else: we don't collect location data, contacts, device identifiers for advertising, or any analytics/tracking SDKs.
⚙️

How it's used

Everything you enter is used only to run the app's core features for your team: tracking the team fund balance, who paid what, game attendance, and splitting practice court costs. Your data is visible only to other members of the same team, based on the role your team assigns you.

🚫

What we don't do

TeamKitty never processes real payments — it's a record-keeping tool only; money changes hands outside the app (Venmo, cash, etc.). We don't sell or share your data with third parties, and we don't run ads.

🗄️

Where data is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (a hosted Postgres database provider) with access controls limiting each person to their own team's data. Receipt photos are stored in a private file bucket, accessible only to signed-in members of your team.

🔑

Your choices

You can change your display name anytime from within the app. To delete your account entirely, see our account deletion page.
