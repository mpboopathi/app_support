About TeamKitty

TeamKitty is a team-finances and scheduling app for small sports club teams — built to replace the group-chat spreadsheet with one shared, always-up-to-date source of truth.

Key features:

  • Shared ledger — members submit expenses and payments; officers review and acknowledge them, so the team fund balance is always accurate and auditable.
  • Season tracking — registration fees, league fees, and per-game costs rolled up into one season summary.
  • Game attendance — track who played each game and tie it back to registration-fee return calculations.
  • Practice sessions — book a practice, split the court cost evenly, and settle up with a simple claim/confirm flow.
  • Roster & officers — manage who's on the team and who holds treasurer/captain/board roles.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is TeamKitty free?
Yes — TeamKitty is free to download and use.

What do I need to get started?
Create an account with your email address, then either create a new team or join one with a team's invite code.

Does TeamKitty handle real payments?
No. TeamKitty is a record-keeping tool only — money changes hands outside the app (Venmo, cash, etc.) and you log it afterward so everyone can see the team fund balance.

Who can see our team's data?
Only members of your own team. Every team's roster, ledger, and practice data is kept fully separate from every other team on TeamKitty.
