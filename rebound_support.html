About Rebound

Rebound is an AI-powered job search companion designed for people navigating a career transition — whether you've been laid off, want to pivot, or are simply ready for something new.

Key features:

  • AI Resume Analysis — upload your resume and get instant, personalized feedback on how to improve it
  • Smart Job Search — find relevant jobs and tailor your resume to match each listing
  • Application Tracker — manage every application from one place, from applied to offer
  • Interview Prep Coach — practice answers to common and role-specific questions
  • Daily Check-In — emotional support and momentum to keep your search on track

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Rebound free?
Yes — Rebound is free to download and use.

What do I need to get started?
Just create an account with your email address. No credit card required.

How does AI resume analysis work?
Upload a PDF or paste your resume text. Our AI evaluates it against current hiring standards and provides a skills inventory, strengths, gaps, and actionable improvement suggestions.

How does interview prep work?
Once you have a phone screen or interview scheduled in your tracker, tap "Prep Interview" to get a tailored list of likely questions and guidance on how to answer them effectively.

Is my data private?
Yes. Your resume and job data are stored securely and never shared. See our Privacy Policy.
